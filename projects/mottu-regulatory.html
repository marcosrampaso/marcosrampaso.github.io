Mottu Mexico · Regulatory operations

Fleet registration & document reissue

I work directly with the Mexico operations team to build and maintain .NET services, browser automations, and the internal tools used to run vehicle registration and document workflows.

Registration and payments

I build and maintain workflows for first-time plate applications, document reissue, and CURP validation. This includes national vehicle registry checks before first-plate applications, REST integrations with internal services, and automated payment file generation.

From May through October 2026, the registration and reissue workflows I maintain in Mexico handled approximately 1,750 motorcycles and 1,040 payments per month on average.

Moving document reissue out of Pipefy

I built an idempotent migration with dry-run validation and moved 635 cases from Pipefy into Mottu’s internal workflow. I integrated DHL shipment tracking and automated eligible approvals using license plate OCR.

I also redesigned the RPA workflow, lowering average robot submissions from 2.54 to 1.02 per reissue request, a reduction of about 60%.

Monitoring workflows in production

I built Datadog dashboards, alerts, and a service-level objective using Terraform. I added metrics for regulatory backlogs, payment failures, and integration health across Brazil and Mexico. I also build internal monitoring screens and contribute to automated tests, code reviews, and CI/CD for applications deployed with Docker and Kubernetes.
